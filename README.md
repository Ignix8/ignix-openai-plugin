# Ignix for ChatGPT and Codex

Ignix is an all-in-one social media manager for creators, small businesses and agencies. Connect your accounts once and run your social media in plain words: plan and schedule posts, make reels, answer messages, automate Instagram DMs and see what is working.

Social media scheduler: schedule posts, carousels, Reels and Stories on 13 networks (Instagram, Facebook, TikTok, YouTube, LinkedIn, X/Twitter, Threads, Pinterest, Bluesky, Reddit, Google Business Profile, Telegram and Discord) from one content calendar, with a posting queue and the best times to post.

AI content in your brand voice: captions, hashtags, post ideas and images, and Autopilot, which drafts a month of content from what works in your niche. Turn your best posts into new content for other networks.

Reels and short videos: make Reels, TikToks and Shorts with AI voice-over, subtitles, sound effects and AI video clips, or edit your own footage into clips with subtitles, hooks and zooms and cut the best moments from a long video. Editing and rendering run on your computer with Ignix Editor.

Competitor and niche research: analyze any public Instagram, TikTok or YouTube account, see which posts beat its usual numbers, find the top reels in your niche and look up the numbers behind any reel.

Instagram DM automation: comment-to-DM (send a link by DM when someone comments a keyword), story replies, lead capture and a chatbot builder for Instagram, Facebook Messenger, WhatsApp, Telegram and website live chat. Sparky, your AI agent, answers customers around the clock from your website and FAQ. Broadcasts and drip sequences.

Unified inbox: DMs, comments, mentions and reviews from every account in one place, with a CRM, tags, team assignment and approvals. Replies are drafted for you to approve.

Social media analytics: reach, engagement, follower growth and top posts per network, explained in plain language, with a shareable client report and social listening for brand mentions and sentiment.

Link in bio store and booking: sell digital products and courses from your own link-in-bio page, and take appointments synced to Google Calendar.

Agencies: one workspace per client.

Nothing is published or sent without your OK. You need an Ignix account and at least one connected social account; Instagram automations need an Instagram professional (Business or Creator) account.

## Skills

| Skill | What it does |
|---|---|
| `content-plan` | Plans a week, a campaign or a month of posts in your brand voice and leaves them ready as drafts or scheduled |
| `reels` | Makes Reels, TikToks and Shorts with voice-over, subtitles and AI video, or edits your own footage into clips |
| `performance-report` | Reads your analytics and explains, in plain language, what works and what to do next |
| `inbox` | Works through your messages, comments and reviews, drafting replies you approve before anything is sent |
| `comment-to-dm` | Builds "comment a keyword, get the link by DM" automations and other chatbot flows |
| `repurpose` | Finds your best posts and turns them into new content for other networks |

## How to use

- "Plan next week's Instagram and TikTok posts for my coffee shop and schedule them at the best times."
- "Make a 20-second reel announcing our new menu, with voice-over and subtitles."
- "When someone comments GUIDE on my next post, DM them the link to my free guide."
- "How did my accounts perform this month? Which post brought the most followers?"
- "Show me unread DMs and comments from all my accounts and draft replies."
- "Analyze @competitor on Instagram and tell me which of their posts beat their usual numbers."

## Install

Find **Ignix** in the Plugins directory in ChatGPT or Codex, install it and sign in to your Ignix account.
